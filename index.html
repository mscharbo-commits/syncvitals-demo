How the demo works: a shared data engine (data-engine.js) ticks every 8 seconds, evolving each patient's vitals and recomputing risk scores. Command Dashboard and Prediction AI can each push their own richer, disease-specific analysis on top of that baseline — whichever one you have open "wins" the narrative, but every portal is always reading the same underlying numbers. For the best demo, open Command Dashboard first, then open Nurse Portal in a second tab and watch the risk panel update on its own.
