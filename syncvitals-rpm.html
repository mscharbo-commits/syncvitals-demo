Days 1–30: Getting Stable
Right now — first priority
›
Your blood pressure is too high right now. The most important thing this month is to get it under control. Follow these steps every day and check in with your nurse weekly.
📏 Checking your blood pressure
Take your BP twice a day — once in the morning before any medication, once in the evening. Sit quietly for 5 minutes first.
Weigh yourself every morning before eating or drinking. Write it down — tell your nurse if you gain 2 or more pounds in one day.
💊 Your medication
Take your Lisinopril every day at the same time. Set a phone alarm if it helps. Never skip it or take two at once.
Do not take ibuprofen (Advil) or naproxen (Aleve) for pain — they raise blood pressure and harm your kidneys. Use Tylenol instead. Ask your nurse if you are unsure.
🍽️ What to eat
Less salt — avoid adding salt at the table or in cooking. Skip canned soups, deli meats, fast food, chips, and frozen meals this month. These have hidden salt that raises your BP.
More fruits and vegetables — try to eat 4–5 servings a day. Bananas, sweet potatoes, and spinach are especially helpful for blood pressure.
Write down everything you eat in a food diary — even if it's just a note on your phone. Share it with your nurse at your weekly call.
Drink no more than 6–8 cups of fluid a day (water, tea, soup all count) — this protects your kidneys.
🚶 Moving your body
Walk for 5–10 minutes, twice a day at a slow, comfortable pace. It is okay to sit and rest every few minutes.
Sit down for tasks like brushing your teeth, combing your hair, or folding laundry — this saves your energy and protects your heart.
Do not exercise when your BP is above 170 — rest and recheck in 30 minutes.
📅 Your appointments this month
📞 This week: Nurse Santos calls you to check on your BP and answer questions
📞 Every week: Weekly check-in call with Nurse Santos
🩺 Day 14: Dr. Osei reviews your Lisinopril dose — CKD check
📋 Day 30: Full review — how are you doing? What's working?
Your 30-day goal
Blood pressure reliably below 160 · Taking medication every day · Writing down your food · Walking daily

Days 31–60: Getting Better
Build on your progress
›
Great job reaching Day 30! Now it's time to build on what's working, go a little further with diet and exercise, and start learning about your conditions so you can take even more control.
📏 Monitoring — a little easier now
If your BP has been below 155 for two weeks, you can check it once a day in the morning — your nurse will confirm this.
Still weigh yourself daily — your kidneys can cause fluid to build up quickly. Tell your nurse about any 2-pound gain in 48 hours.
Day 45 blood test: Your doctor will check your kidney function (GFR) and potassium. No fasting needed — ask your nurse about scheduling.
🍽️ Eating — going further
Meet with your dietitian at Day 35 — they'll help you create a meal plan you actually enjoy, not one that feels like punishment.
Add low-fat dairy to your day — a yogurt in the morning or a glass of skim milk. Calcium helps lower blood pressure.
Stop sugary drinks — soda, juice, sweet tea. Replace with water or unsweetened herbal tea. This also helps your weight and kidneys.
🚶 Moving more
Try to walk 15–20 minutes a day — you can still take rest breaks every 5–10 minutes. You've earned this increase!
On days when walking is hard, try seated exercises — lift your legs while sitting, roll your shoulders, squeeze your hands open and closed.
📚 Learning about your health
Complete the hypertension education module in your SyncVitals app — it's short (15 minutes) and explains why your BP affects your kidneys.
Write down your 3 personal health goals — what does feeling better mean to you? Share them with Nurse Santos.
📅 Your appointments — Days 31–60
📞 Every 2 weeks: Check-in call with Nurse Santos
🍎 Day 35: Dietitian appointment — personalized meal plan
🩺 Day 45: Dr. Osei — kidney lab results & medication review
📋 Day 60: Review — see how far you've come
Your 60-day goal
Blood pressure below 140/90 · Longer daily walks · Dietitian meal plan in place · Know your target numbers by heart

Days 61–90: Staying Well
Make these changes your new normal
›
You've worked so hard. This final phase is about turning everything you've learned into habits that last for life — not just for 90 days.
📏 Monitoring — maintenance
If BP is stable, check it 3 times per week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday). Your doctor will decide if you can reduce monitoring after Day 75.
Continue weekly weight check — still important for your kidneys, even when things are going well.
Day 80 blood test: Kidney function, electrolytes, and cholesterol. These results show how your kidneys have responded to 3 months of care.
🏃 Staying active
Aim for 30 minutes of light activity most days — you can split this into two 15-minute walks. You have been building up to this all along.
Ask Dr. Osei about adding gentle resistance exercises (light bands or weights) — only if cleared by your doctor.
🫘 Protecting your kidneys for the long term
Every 10-point drop in your top blood pressure number slows down kidney damage. You are protecting your kidneys every day you stay on track.
Never take Advil, Aleve, or aspirin without asking your care team — even "natural" supplements can harm your kidneys. Always check first.
Keep eating low-sodium, high-vegetable meals — this is now your lifestyle, not a temporary diet. Your kidneys will thank you.
🏠 Your emergency plan
Make sure a family member or friend knows your emergency plan — what number is your crisis threshold, what are the warning symptoms, who to call.
Review and complete the Day 90 self-management checklist with Nurse Santos — this celebrates your progress and plans your next steps.
📅 Your appointments — Days 61–90
📞 Every 2 weeks: Nurse check-in — shorter now, you're doing great
🩺 Day 75: Dr. Osei — check if you qualify for reduced monitoring
🔬 Day 80: Labs — kidneys, cholesterol, electrolytes
🎉 Day 90: Graduation review — how far you've come in 3 months!

Blood pressure below 135/85 · Managing your own diet & exercise · Kidneys stable or improving · Ready to teach someone else your healthy habits!
